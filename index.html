Frequently Asked Questions

How does Dase operate without databases?
All tools are designed to run client-side in your browser or redirect directly to third-party endpoints. Your preferences (like theme and pinned cards) are saved locally on your device via localStorage.
Are there any hidden costs or subscriptions?
None. Every feature and tool across the Dase ecosystem is completely free for everyone.
Do I need to create an account?
No account or login is required. You can use all tools immediately upon opening the page.
